## About the Opportunity

The Enterprise Systems Governance & Enablement Manager will establish and lead an enterprise-wide approach to maximise the value, effectiveness and adoption of Jewish Care’s enterprise systems.

Reporting to the Head of Data & Analytics, this role will provide enterprise coordination, governance and business enablement across operational teams, Product Owners, IT, Data & Analytics, vendors and senior stakeholders.

The role will establish a consistent approach to identifying, assessing and prioritising significant system enhancements, workflow improvements and technology-enabled process changes. It will provide an enterprise view of competing priorities, risks, dependencies and opportunities, ensuring that improvement activity is aligned to organisational priorities and delivers clear business outcomes.

 

## Key Responsibilities

- Establish and maintain enterprise systems governance, prioritisation and operating frameworks.
- Develop and maintain a consolidated enterprise view of system capability, risks, utilisation, improvement opportunities and priorities.
- Establish and manage the enterprise enhancement and improvement intake and backlog, ensuring significant initiatives are appropriately assessed and prioritised.
- Partner with operational leaders and Product Owners to translate business needs into practical system and process improvements.
- Identify opportunities to reduce duplication, manual workarounds, process variation and unnecessary customisation.
- Lead or facilitate process reviews, workflow improvements, business analysis and current/future-state process mapping.
- Coordinate business readiness, user acceptance testing and adoption activities for significant system changes.
- Establish appropriate documentation, knowledge management and super-user/operational champion networks.
- Work closely with Data & Analytics to consider data quality, reporting, integration and benefits realisation.
- Maintain enterprise improvement backlogs and roadmaps, tracking risks, dependencies, decisions and outcomes.
- Build effective relationships with technology vendors and work with IT on system dependencies, integrations and support pathways.
- Provide regular reporting and recommendations to governance forums and senior stakeholders.

The role will progressively expand its system coverage over the first 6–12 months, with priorities agreed with the Head of Data & Analytics and relevant executive stakeholders.

## About You

We are seeking an experienced enterprise system, transformation, business improvement or technology enablement professional who can operate confidently across a complex, multi-stakeholder environment.

You will bring:

- Demonstrated experience across enterprise business systems, digital transformation, business improvement or systems enablement.
- Strong understanding of the relationship between business processes, technology and organisational outcomes.
- Experience establishing or improving governance, prioritisation and operating models.
- Strong analytical, problem-solving and process improvement capability.
- Experience managing competing priorities and influencing across organisational boundaries.
- Strong executive, stakeholder engagement, facilitation and communication skills.
- Experience supporting system enhancements, releases, implementations and business readiness.
- Understanding of data quality, reporting and information-management impacts arising from system change.
- A pragmatic enterprise minded approach, with the ability to balance immediate operational needs with sustainable enterprise outcomes.
