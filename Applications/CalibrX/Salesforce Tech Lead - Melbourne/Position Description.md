# Salesforce Tech Lead - Melbourne
## Company: CalibrX
## Hiring Manager: Garth Kharitou

## Position Description
CalibrX is looking for a Salesforce Technical Lead to join a large enterprise environment in Melbourne. This is a hybrid Technical Lead / delivery-focused position, combining Salesforce technical leadership with sprint execution, team facilitation and developer coaching.

This isn’t a role where you’ll spend your entire week writing Apex. Around 60% of the role is focused on delivery leadership and keeping the team moving, with around 40% focused on hands-on technical leadership including POCs, technical design, code reviews and helping developers solve complex issues.

You’ll work across a modern Salesforce environment incorporating Service Cloud, MuleSoft, Agentforce, AI, voice and complex integrations.

## Responsibilities:
- Lead the day-to-day technical delivery of Salesforce projects and sprint execution
- Run daily stand-ups, manage blockers and ensure the development team delivers against sprint commitments
- Provide technical direction across Salesforce development, configuration, integrations and automation
- Build POCs and technical spikes to de-risk complex solutions before development
- Review Apex, configuration, Flow and integration solutions to maintain strong development standards
- Work closely with Salesforce and MuleSoft developers to ensure integrations and API contracts are aligned
- Coach developers and BAs and help improve technical capability across the team
- Work with BAs to ensure stories are technically ready before entering a sprint
- Champion a configuration-first, low-code/no-code approach while understanding when custom development is required

## Experience:
- Strong experience working as a Salesforce Technical Lead, Lead Developer or Senior Salesforce Consultant
- Excellent understanding of Salesforce architecture, development and platform best practices
- Strong hands-on experience with Apex, Flow, configuration and integrations
- Experience reviewing code and providing technical guidance to Salesforce developers
- Good understanding of APIs and working alongside integration teams, ideally MuleSoft
- Experience working within Agile delivery teams and comfortable driving sprint execution
- Strong communication skills and the ability to unblock technical teams quickly
- Exposure to Agentforce, AI, Service Cloud Voice or contact centre technology would be highly regarded
- Comfortable balancing technical leadership with delivery and team facilitation

## Why you should apply:
- Work across a large and complex Salesforce environment
- Exposure to Agentforce, AI, voice and modern integration technologies
- Take ownership of technical delivery rather than simply producing technical designs
- Opportunity to coach and influence a strong Salesforce delivery team
