# Head of Engineering
## Company: Compass Education
## Hiring Manager: 

## About the job
Come shape the future of education with us. 

At Compass, we're on a mission to transform the school day for everyone - from staff and students to families and administrators. We build smart, seamless technology that empowers schools to focus on what really matters: learning, growing and thriving.

That mission has fuelled our growth into a global scale-up, now supporting 5,000+ schools across three countries, backed by a team of 300+ people. Our all-in-one school management platform is redefining how education communities connect, communicate and operate.

We're now looking for a Head of Engineering to own end-to-end engineering delivery and the day-to-day people leadership of our engineering org, spanning Compass, SchoolBytes, Griddle and Clipboard.

## About The Role

Reporting to the CPTO, you'll translate technology and product priorities into predictable engineering execution, build and develop a strong Development Manager cohort, and own the engineering operating model, organisational capability and delivery performance.

This is a leadership role built around the Development Manager layer - you'll lead through 5-7 Development Managers rather than directly managing squads, and be accountable for the consistency, capability and execution of the wider engineering organisation of :40-50 engineers.

## What You'll Do

### Engineering Delivery & Operations

- Own engineering's delivery performance across all squads, partnering with Product to ensure capacity, priorities and roadmap commitments are realistic, transparent and aligned. 
- Establish a consistent engineering operating model, spanning planning, estimation and release practices, that enables teams to plan, prioritise and deliver predictably at scale. 
- Own engineering capacity planning and allocation across squads, balancing strategic priorities, delivery commitments and team capability
- Establish and monitor meaningful engineering performance metrics, covering roadmap predictability, cycle time, quality, reliability technical debt and team health and report on these to the CPTO and ELT. 
- Identify delivery risks and dependencies early, driving resolution or escalating material trade-offs to the CPTO


### People Leadership

- Directly manage and develop the Development Manager cohort. Set clear expectations, coach on people leadership and hold them accountable for their squads' delivery and team health. 
- Own engineering headcount planning, hiring and onboarding in partnership with the CPTO and People & Culture. 
- Continuously assess engineering team structures, skills and capabilities to ensure the organisation is designed for current and future priorities. 
- Build engineering career pathways and capability frameworks that support progression for engineers and managers. 
- Own engineering engagement and retention, monitoring team health signals and championing a strong engineering culture


### Engineering Practice & Standards

- Partner with technical leaders (Development Managers, Tech Leads and the Head of Technology) to ensure engineering decisions support scalability, maintainability, security, reliability and delivery outcomes - you'll own engineering quality and technical execution even though this role is not the ultimate architecture owner. 
- Champion consistent engineering standards, tooling and ways of working across squads. 
- Balance delivery pace against technical debt and quality, escalating trade-offs to the CPTO where they affect strategy or architecture
- Partner with the Head of Technology to ensure engineering teams implement and maintain required standards for reliability security and compliance across AU, UK and Ireland


### Stakeholder Partnership

- Act as engineering's day-to-day representative to Product, People & Culture, the - Head of Technology and other functional leaders. 
- Give the CPTO clear, timely visibility into delivery status, risks and people matters. 


## Requirements
### About You

This role is for an experienced engineering leader who's managed through managers across multiple teams in a product-led SaaS environment - someone who measures success by the strength of the team they've built, backed by strong delivery, people leadership and commercial judgement.

### You will bring:

- Experience leading through engineering managers across multiple teams, with accountability for delivery, people and organisational performance
- 10+ years experience in software engineering, including several years leading Engineering/Development Managers in a product or SaaS environment; the shape of that experience matters more than an exact number of years. 
- A track record of owning delivery outcomes across multiple teams to squads, ideally in a product-led SaaS, PE-backed or similarly performance-oriented environment where predictability and efficiency are closely tracked. 
- Experience scaling an engineering organisation and leading through change or transformation, including building consistent practice from process immaturity. 
- Strong people leadership - experience coaching and developing engineering managers, not just individual engineers. 
- Strong commercial and resource-management capability, comfortable owning capacity planning, headcount trade-offs and organisational design decisions
- Ability to operate effectively with Product leadership, aligning delivery capacity and commitments against roadmap priorities. 
- Experience using engineering metrics (delivery, quality, reliability, team health) to drive performance, not just report on it. 
- Sufficient technical depth to engage credibly with Compass' engineering stack and technical leaders, and to understand trade-offs relating to scalability, maintainability, security and reliability. 

### Highly regarded:

- Experience across distributed or multi-entity engineering teams; AU/UK/Ireland exposure is a plus given Compass' footprint
- Direct experience with C#/.NET, React.js, Node.js and Typescript.
