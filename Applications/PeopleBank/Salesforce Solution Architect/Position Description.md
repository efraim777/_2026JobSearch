## About the job

An exciting opportunity exists for an experienced Senior Salesforce Solution Architect to join a large-scale federal government program on an initial 12-month labour hire contract.

This role is ideal for a highly skilled architect who can bridge business requirements with technical solutions, provide architectural leadership, and drive best-practice design across complex Salesforce ecosystems.
 
## As the selected candidate, you will:
- Collaborate with Product Managers and Delivery Teams to develop product roadmaps and plan solution design activities, including technical dependencies and delivery sequencing.
- Translate business requirements into scalable Salesforce solutions and produce detailed architecture, design documents, and technical artefacts.
- Define and design non-functional requirements including performance, scalability, security, resilience, observability, and recoverability.
- Provide technical leadership, mentorship, and architectural guidance to solution architects, developers, and project teams.
- Participate in architecture governance, technical review boards, and executive-level solution endorsement processes.
- Ensure solution designs align with Salesforce CRM architecture, product roadmaps, industry best practices, and organisational architectural standards.
- Drive consistency through architecture standards, frameworks, and design processes across the broader architecture team.
- Work within a Scaled Agile Framework (SAFe), contributing to PI Planning, ART events, sprint planning, and other agile ceremonies.
- Champion continuous improvement initiatives that improve delivery quality, efficiency, and business outcomes.

## To be successful in this role, you should have:
- Minimum 8 years' experience in a Solution Architect role delivering Salesforce projects, including large-scale and complex enterprise implementations.
- Extensive experience designing enterprise-grade non-functional requirements, including performance, scalability, resilience, observability, recoverability, and security.
- Deep architectural and technical expertise across Salesforce platforms, including multiple Salesforce Clouds and industry solutions.
- Strong hands-on experience with Salesforce and Vlocity/OmniStudio.
- Proven experience designing integration architectures and patterns between Salesforce and external enterprise systems.
- Ability to develop proof of concepts and prototypes to validate and demonstrate proposed solutions.
- Demonstrated success driving continuous improvement initiatives that enhance delivery quality, efficiency, and speed.
- Exceptional stakeholder engagement, communication, and relationship management skills, with the ability to articulate complex technical concepts, risks, and recommendations to both technical and non-technical audiences.
- Hold an NV1 security clearance or be eligible to obtain one.

It is desirable if you have:
Salesforce Architect certifications.
Vlocity/OmniStudio certifications.
A proactive, solutions-focused mindset with a strong "can-do" attitude.
Experience or passion for accessible and inclusive design and development practices.
If you meet these criteria and are eager to make a meaningful impact through your expertise, we encourage you to apply.
