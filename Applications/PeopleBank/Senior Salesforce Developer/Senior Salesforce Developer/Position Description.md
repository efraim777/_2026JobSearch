# Senior Salesforce Developer

## About the job
We are sourcing highly skilled Senior Salesforce Developerto work with a leading Australian Government organisation

The role is initially contracted for 12 months, with an option to extend for a further 12 months.

## As the selected candidate, you will:

- Develop, test, and support Salesforce solutions, including Lightning Web Components (LWC), Experience Cloud, Apex, and platform configurations.
- Collaborate with business analysts, Solution Architects, and delivery teams to translate business requirements into high-quality, scalable technical solutions.
- Contribute to developing secure, accessible, and maintainable digital services aligned with platform standards and user needs.
- Participate in code reviews, testing, release processes, and continuous improvement initiatives to optimise quality and delivery.
- Support integration activities between Salesforce and external systems through APIs and established patterns.
- Utilise modern development tools and practices to improve productivity and ensure efficient delivery.

## To be successful in this role you should have:
- Demonstrated experience developing Salesforce-based solutions within enterprise environments.
- Strong technical knowledge and hands-on experience with Lightning Web Components, Experience Cloud, Apex, and Salesforce platform configuration.
- Proven ability to develop integrations between Salesforce and external systems using APIs.
- Experience delivering accessible digital services, applying accessibility standards, and inclusive design principles.
- Familiarity with source control, automated deployment, and modern software development practices.
- Excellent collaboration and communication skills to work effectively within multidisciplinary teams and stakeholders.

## It is desirable if you have:
- Salesforce certifications such as Administrator, Platform App Builder, or Platform Developer I.
- Experience with Salesforce Industries (Vlocity), including Integration Procedures and OmniStudio.
- Background supporting customer-facing digital services within government or regulated environments.
- Knowledge of other Salesforce platform capabilities like Service Cloud and associated digital service platforms.
