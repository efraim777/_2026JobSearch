# Senior Salesforce Consultant — Not for Profit
## About SalesFix
SalesFix is one of Australia's longest-standing privately owned Salesforce consulting partners. We work with mid-market and enterprise organisations across Not for Profit, Aged Care, B2B Commercial and Government to deliver Salesforce implementations, agentic operations programmes, and fractional AI advisory services. We are a small, senior team that punches well above our weight, and we are building the AI-native practice of the future.

We are not a body shop. We do not carry a bench. Every person here does work that requires judgement, relationships, or accountability. That is the standard we hire to.

## The Role
Australia's Not for Profit sector is at an inflection point. Demand is rising while funding tightens, Salesforce ended NPSP feature development in 2023 and now points every new organisation to Agentforce Nonprofit, and only 14% of Australian not-for-profits hold an AI policy of any kind. Thousands know they have to move. Very few know how, in what order, or what it will cost them to get it wrong.

That is where this role sits. Every engagement you lead is a child's potential unleashed, a family kept together, an elder living with dignity, because someone in the room listened properly and asked the harder question.

You own the business outcome across our Not for Profit client base, where a single constituent might be a donor, program participant, volunteer and case all at once. The Configurators and Developers build. Your job is to make sure they are building the right thing.

Reporting to the Squad Leader, Projects, alongside our Not for Profit Practice Lead and Solution Architects. Australia based, Melbourne, Sydney or Brisbane preferred.

## What You Will Own
### Consult and challenge
You run discovery with executives, fundraising and program managers, getting to the business outcome behind the stated requirement before anyone agrees to build. You drive competing agendas to a decision. You tell the kind truth, pushing back when a request will not deliver value or costs more than the problem is worth, and you always bring a better option. You enter the danger: the misaligned sponsor, the contested process, the decision the customer keeps deferring.

### Deep fluency across both nonprofit ecosystems
Hands-on Agentforce Nonprofit across Fundraising, Program Management, Case Management and Outcome Management, plus genuine NPSP experience. You know that NPSP to Agentforce Nonprofit is a full reimplementation, not an upgrade, and you are honest about when a client should move and when they should hold. Your readiness assessments turn a daunting migration into a staged, funded roadmap the board can approve.

### Put AI to work, responsibly
You know where agents earn their place across the fundraising, program and case lifecycle, and where they do not. You design for human-in-the-loop from the start, because in a sector handling health, disability and vulnerability data, autonomy without oversight is not an option. You hold the governance conversation at board level: consent, privacy, model boundaries and what happens when the agent is wrong. Most clients have no policy at all and will look to you first.

### Redesign the business, not just the system
You redesign processes rather than automating existing inefficiency, and you build the data and outcomes foundation deliberately, because reporting to funders and regulators is why many of these programs keep their funding. You design so compliance with the Aged Care Act, SIRS, NDIS, DEX and child safe standards is a by-product of good process, not a burden bolted on at the end. You reach for proven IP first, and you own requirements through to build-ready stories and acceptance criteria.

### Lead the engagement
You own the scope, effort and commercial health of your engagements, catching scope change early and converting it into agreed scope or a clear decision not to proceed. You verify delivered work before it reaches the customer, and you support UAT, training and go-live so the solution lands with the people who have to use it.

### Cultivate excellence
You sharpen solution design and scoping across the Delivery team, keep your own edge on Data Cloud and agentic AI, and turn field learning into reusable SalesFix IP.
