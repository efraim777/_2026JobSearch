## We bring the energy!

Through purpose and people - not just through the infrastructure we operate, but through the way we show up for each other, our customers’ and our communities. The future of energy is in our hands. Let’s shape it together.

Go beyond keeping the lights on. Solve energy challenges that matter most.

We have an opportunity for a Process Automation Lead to join our team. As the Process Automation Lead, you’ll lead a small team of Process Automation Specialists to map, simplify, re-engineer and automate how work is performed across AusNet. You’ll enforce the standards for process analysis and automation delivery, prioritise and allocate your team’s capacity against the highest value opportunities, and run a disciplined delivery cadence.

Combining deep process expertise with hands-on automation capability and people leadership, you’ll reduce manual effort, improve quality and create measurable business value using technologies such as Microsoft Power Automate and emerging AI-enabled automation and process management / mapping tools.

## What you’ll be doing:

- Lead and develop a team of three Process Automation Specialists, building capability in process analysis, re-engineering and automation.

- Manage the automation pipeline, prioritising work based on value, risk, urgency and team capacity.

- Drive delivery through effective backlog management, planning, reviews and continuous improvement.

- Maintain process mapping, re-engineering and automation standards aligned to Enterprise Process Management frameworks.

- Act as the senior contact for process automation, reporting on priorities, performance and realised benefits.

- Partner with stakeholders to analyse current-state processes, identify pain points, waste and improvement opportunities.

- Facilitate workshops to uncover process issues and define automation opportunities.

- Establish a clear understanding of current-state processes, controls, data, dependencies and performance baselines.

- Document current and future-state processes using agreed modelling standards and frameworks.

- Simplify and redesign processes by removing waste, addressing root causes and avoiding automation of inefficient practices.

- Assess and prioritise automation opportunities based on value, feasibility, risk and return on investment.

- Translate business needs into clear requirements, acceptance criteria and solution designs.

- Oversee the design, build, testing, deployment and maintenance of workflow and robotic process automations.

- Ensure security, privacy, compliance, auditability and operational support requirements are embedded throughout the automation lifecycle.

- Lead testing, defect resolution, deployment, training and handover activities.

- Monitor automation performance, adoption and benefits, driving continuous improvement.

- Evaluate emerging AI and automation capabilities and apply them where safe, governed and appropriate.
