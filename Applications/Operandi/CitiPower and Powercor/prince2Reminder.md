# PRINCE2 Project Management Framework

**PRINCE2** (Projects IN Controlled Environments) is a structured project management methodology used globally to manage projects of all sizes and types. Its core foundation relies on 7 Principles (the "why"), 7 Themes (the "what"), and 7 Processes (the "how") that guide a project from start to finish. The methodology focuses heavily on establishing a justifiable business case, ensuring that the project remains worthwhile and aligned with corporate strategy throughout its entire lifecycle.

A central strength of PRINCE2 is its emphasis on defined roles and responsibilities, dividing project governance into manageable layers like the Project Board and the Project Manager. It promotes management by exception, meaning boundaries for time, cost, and quality are set, and senior management is only involved if these tolerances are breached. By breaking projects down into controlled, sequential stages, PRINCE2 provides a flexible framework that improves risk management, ensures strict quality control, and encourages continuous learning from experience.

---

## The 7 Principles
These are the guiding rules that must be followed for a project to be considered a true PRINCE2 project.

*   **Continued Business Justification:** Every project must have a clear return on investment.
*   **Learn from Experience:** Teams must document and apply lessons from previous projects.
*   **Defined Roles and Responsibilities:** Everyone must understand exactly what they are accountable for.
*   **Manage by Stages:** Projects must be planned and monitored phase by phase.
*   **Manage by Exception:** Managers only escalate issues when agreed boundaries are breached.
*   **Focus on Products:** Teams prioritize output quality over the activities used to create them.
*   **Tailor to Suit the Project:** The framework must be adapted to fit the project's size and environment.

---

## The 7 Themes
These represent the project management disciplines that must be continuously addressed throughout the project.

*   **Business Case:** Focuses on whether the project remains worthwhile and viable.
*   **Organization:** Defines the structure of the project management team.
*   **Quality:** Ensures the final deliverables meet the required standards.
*   **Plans:** Outlines how the project objectives will be achieved.
*   **Risk:** Identifies and manages uncertainties that could threaten success.
*   **Change:** Assesses and controls requests for modifications to the project scope.
*   **Progress:** Monitors performance to see if the project is on track.

---

## The 7 Processes
These are the sequential steps that guide the project from the initial idea to final closure.

1.  **Starting Up a Project:** Ensures the project is viable before spending money.
2.  **Directing a Project:** Enables senior management to maintain overall control and make key decisions.
3.  **Initiating a Project:** Establishes a solid plan and baseline for the project's execution.
4.  **Controlling a Stage:** Covers day-to-day management of work and monitoring of progress.
5.  **Managing Product Delivery:** Coordinates the creation and handover of actual project deliverables.
6.  **Managing a Stage Boundary:** Reviews the current stage and plans the next phase for approval.
7.  **Closing a Project:** Decommissions the project, hands over results, and captures final lessons.

## PRINCE2 vs. Agile

While PRINCE2 focuses on project governance and control, Agile is an approach focused on speed, flexibility, and continuous product delivery. They are fundamentally different but highly complementary frameworks.

### Comparison Table

| Feature | PRINCE2 (Traditional) | Agile |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Core Philosophy** | Control and predictability | Flexibility and speed |
| **Planning Approach** | Upfront, phase-by-phase planning | Iterative, adaptive planning |
| **Primary Focus** | Project governance and business justification | Continuous product delivery and user value |
| **Management Style** | Managed by exception through strict hierarchies | Self-organising, collaborative teams |
| **Handling Change** | Rigid change control processes | Welcomes change as user needs evolve |
| **Project Boundary** | Clear beginning, middle, and end | Fluid cycles (Sprints) with ongoing product evolution |

### Summary of Key Differences

*   **Governance vs. Execution:** PRINCE2 defines who makes decisions and why the project exists (governance). Agile focuses on how the team builds and refines the actual product (execution).
*   **Fixed vs. Flexed Elements:** In PRINCE2, the scope is usually fixed, while time and budget are managed within strict tolerances. In Agile, the time and budget are fixed (e.g., a 2-week sprint), while the scope is flexed based on what the team can realistically finish.
*   **The "PRINCE2 Agile" Hybrid:** Because they solve different problems, they are often used together. PRINCE2 handles the overarching business justification, budgets, and corporate reporting, while the development team uses Agile methods (like Scrum or Kanban) to build the product incrementally.
