## About State Trustees

At State Trustees, we support Victorians from all walks of life with their financial and legal affairs, particularly during life's most important and challenging moments.

As a Victorian Government-backed organisation, we provide trusted services that help people plan, protect, and manage their affairs with confidence, ensuring peace of mind when it matters most.

## About the role

State Trustees is investing in technology that helps our people deliver better outcomes for Victorians. We're looking for a Salesforce DevOps Engineer to help keep our Salesforce platform secure, reliable and performing at its best.

This is a broad technical role combining Salesforce administration, platform operations and DevOps, with a strong focus on automation, environments, releases and continuous improvement.

## What You'll Do

You'll help maintain and evolve our Salesforce platform while enabling teams to deliver new capabilities efficiently and reliably.

- Manage Salesforce administration, configuration and platform security
- Support environments, releases, deployments and CI/CD pipelines
- Monitor platform performance and resolve operational issues
- Identify automation opportunities and improve platform efficiency
- Partner with stakeholders to deliver effective Salesforce solutions
- Strengthen governance, DevOps practices and operational standards

## About You

You're a Salesforce professional who enjoys working across platform operations, technology and business needs. You're comfortable troubleshooting issues, improving processes and finding ways to make a platform more reliable and effective. You'll bring:

- Proven experience supporting a mature Salesforce environment
- Salesforce Administrator, Advanced Administrator or equivalent certification
- Strong Salesforce configuration, security, Flow and automation experience
- Experience with environments, releases and deployment processes
- Experience with monitoring, incident management and operational support
- Experience with Salesforce DevOps, CI/CD and source control
- Strong analytical, problem-solving and stakeholder skills
- Experience with tools such as Copado or Gearset, Salesforce integrations and multiple Salesforce environments would be advantageous.
