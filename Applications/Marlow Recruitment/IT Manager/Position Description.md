# IT Manager
## Company: Marlow Recruitment
## Hiring Manager: 

## The Opportunity

Our client requires an experienced IT Manager with strong Salesforce administration skills on an urgent basis to maintain continuity across their IT, systems and data environment.

This is a hands-on interim (fixed-term contract) role covering infrastructure, Salesforce administration, Microsoft 365 and website/application management, reporting directly to the CEO.

## About the Role

Full-time, fixed-term contract

Melbourne, VIC

Immediate start 

Competitive rate negotiable, dependent on experience

You will maintain the organisation's IT network, cloud systems and core infrastructure; manage cybersecurity, backups and business continuity; act as Salesforce Administrator (reports, dashboards, workflows); administer Microsoft 365 (Exchange, SharePoint, OneDrive); oversee the website/CMS; and be the first point of contact for IT queries and vendor management - keeping things running smoothly with minimal handover time.

## About You

Strong IT and systems management experience

Proven Salesforce administration experience essential

Strong Microsoft 365 / SharePoint / cloud infrastructure skills

Strong stakeholder engagement skills, comfortable liaising with CEO, staff, Board and external vendors

Comfortable being hands-on and providing all levels of IT support, from strategic planning through to day-to-day troubleshooting

Comfortable stepping into an existing environment quickly with minimal ramp-up
