## About the job
Senior Salesforce Infrastructure Engineer

🚀 We're looking for a talented Senior Salesforce Infrastructure Engineer to join a high-performing team responsible for managing, supporting, and enhancing a complex Salesforce ecosystem. This role combines hands-on technical expertise with leadership responsibilities, offering the opportunity to influence strategy, improve platform performance, and support critical business initiatives.


What You'll Be Doing

🔹 Lead Salesforce environment planning, administration, and optimisation
 🔹 Manage releases, deployments, environment changes, and CI/CD processes
 🔹 Maintain platform security, governance, and compliance standards
 🔹 Troubleshoot complex Salesforce issues and collaborate with vendor partners
 🔹 Identify and implement automation opportunities across the Salesforce landscape
 🔹 Develop environment and code management strategies supporting project and BAU delivery
 🔹 Provide technical leadership, mentoring, and support to team members
 🔹 Work closely with Developers, Architects, Testers, Cyber Security teams, Business Analysts, and Project Managers


What You'll Bring

✅ Salesforce Administrator Certification
✅ 4+ years' experience within the Salesforce ecosystem
✅ Strong knowledge of Salesforce security and sharing models, including roles, profiles, permissions, OWD, and sharing rules
✅ Experience supporting and administering enterprise Salesforce environments
✅ Hands-on experience with Service Cloud, Data Loader, and Salesforce troubleshooting
✅ Experience managing Salesforce CI/CD processes using GitLab or similar tools
✅ Excellent stakeholder management, communication, and prioritisation skills

Desirable Skills

⭐ ITIL Foundation Certification

⭐ Agile/Scrum delivery experience
