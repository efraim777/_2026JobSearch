# The Role:

The Business Systems Lead provides technical systems leadership across key organisational applications, supporting efficient business operations and ensuring systems meet regulatory obligations.

Working across cloud-based healthcare practice management systems, CRM platforms and other organisational applications, you will lead business process reviews, requirements gathering, system optimisation initiatives, implementations, upgrades and migrations. You will apply technical and business analysis expertise to assess system and application options and provide recommendations to support organisational decision-making.

This role acts as the technical and business analysis backbone for the Digital Steering Committee and serves as the senior technical escalation point for the Health Application Specialist. It is a hands-on technical and business analysis role that combines system administration, project delivery, governance support, stakeholder engagement and continuous improvement



What You’ll Do

Business Systems Administration & Support

Lead day-to-day support for cloud-based healthcare practice management and clinical software systems, including ticket management.
Maintain a clear view of the application landscape to identify duplication and consolidation opportunities.
Requirements Gathering & Process Analysis

Lead requirement gathering and workflow reviews on core clinical software systems.
Produce functional specifications, gap analyses, and recommendations to support procurement and decision-making.
System Implementation & Delivery

Lead the optimisation rollout for cloud-based healthcare practice management systems and the end-to-end CRM implementation, including configuration, data migration, testing, and go-live.
Manage system upgrades and the digital project pipeline.
Data Governance & Quality

Define and maintain CRM data governance standards for quality, segmentation, and retention, in line with privacy legislation.
Vendor & Platform Management

Oversee ongoing CRM administration, including user access, system enhancements, and platform upgrades.
Change Management & Adoption

Prepare change management and training materials to support system transitions and end-user adoption.
Reporting & Analytics Enablement

Align CRM workflows and reporting with business needs.
Work with the National Contact Centre and Marketing team to build CRM dashboards and analytics.
Continuous Improvement

Run post-implementation reviews to capture lessons learned and track benefits realisation.
Governance Support

Prepare architecture assessments and recommendations for the Digital Steering Committee.
Support project managers on charters and status reporting.
People Development

Coach and develop the Health Application Specialist as the senior technical escalation point.
