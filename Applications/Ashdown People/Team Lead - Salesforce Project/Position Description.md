## About the Role
 
An experienced Team Lead with Salesforce knowledge is required to join a Salesforce Flow Migration project. The primary objective of the Salesforce Flow Migration project is the upgrade of the Salesforce applications to the new Salesforce Flow automation technology. This role reports to the Critical Systems Maintenance Program Manager.
 
## Key Qualifications:
- Tertiary qualifications or equivalent professional experience
- Demonstrated experience leading Salesforce projects and upgrades
- Experience in Salesforce Flow automation technology
- Detailed understanding of the migration process from Process Builders and Workflow Rules to Flow Automation.
- Experience in collaborating with and influencing stakeholders to manage expectations and reach agreement.
- Experience in building technical implementation schedules and providing regular status tracking.
- Experience in Atlassian tools including Confluence and Jira is an advantage.

## Key Responsibilities:
 
- Assume the role of Technical Lead for the Sales Flow Migration Project.
- Guide Developers and Test Analysts in the technical migration of Salesforce systems to Salesforce Flow automation technology.
- Ensure that technical quality standards are met at every stage of the project.
- Ensure that Salesforce technical recommendations are followed throughout the project.
- Ensure effective and appropriate utilisation of the Salesforce Flow Migration tool.
- Liaise with project stakeholders, particularly in the Technology Group, regarding technical issues, dependencies, change approvals and code deployments.
- Report technical progress to the Program Manager to ensure that risks, issues and progress can be tracked
