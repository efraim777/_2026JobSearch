# Technical Lead
## Company: The Ronin Consulting
## Hiring Manager: Charles Young
## About the job
Technology LeadNon For Profit – Mental Health SectorMelbourne or SydneyHybrid / RemotePermanent, Full-Time

Our client is looking for a pragmatic, hands-on Technology Lead to help shape and deliver the next phase of its digital and technology roadmap.
This is an opportunity to use technology for genuine social impact—helping improve digital services for people experiencing complex mental health issues and trauma, as well as their families, friends and carers.

Our client is Australia’s leading innovator in digital mental health. Its services include online community, counselling, peer support, groups, forums, telehealth and information services, alongside advocacy and systemic-change work. The organisation is mission-led, ambitious about innovation and committed to designing services informed by lived experience.

This is not a traditional IT-management position. We are looking for a technically credible, commercially minded and naturally curious builder: someone comfortable moving between strategy and delivery, bringing clarity to complexity, and working out what needs to happen next.

## The opportunity
Reporting to the Chief Digital and Operating Officer, the Technology Lead will provide hands-on technical leadership across our client’s digital platforms, Salesforce environment, data, integrations, front-end applications, identity and cloud technology.

You will sit at the centre of a changing technology landscape—working closely with digital, service design, clinical, operational and IT leaders, as well as external vendors and delivery partners. Your role is to turn service needs and roadmap priorities into practical, secure, scalable and supportable technology solutions.

You will be particularly well suited if you enjoy joining the dots across people, platforms and delivery streams—and can bring sound judgement without needing a large team or formal authority beneath you.

## What you’ll do
- Translate the technology roadmap and service priorities into clear technical requirements, solution options, delivery plans and implementation 1priorities.
- Provide practical technical leadership across concurrent initiatives, from early discovery and technical design through to implementation, testing and operational handover.
- Act as the technical bridge across Salesforce, data, integrations, front-end applications, identity, cloud and vendor-delivered solutions.
Shape solution designs, technical tasks, epics, user stories, acceptance criteria and proof-of-concepts to reduce uncertainty and keep delivery moving.
- Clarify integration patterns, data flows, system responsibilities, dependencies and interfaces across a complex technology ecosystem.
- Partner closely with internal stakeholders to ensure technology decisions reflect service needs, user experience, security, privacy, accessibility, scalability and long-term supportability.
- Brief, guide and constructively challenge vendors, contractors and implementation partners to deliver high-quality, fit-for-purpose outcomes.
- Build practical standards around documentation, technical decision-making, knowledge transfer and delivery quality.
- Identify technical risk early, make sensible trade-offs and help our client invest its resources where technology can create the greatest impact.

## The person we’re looking for
- You may currently be a Technical Lead, Solutions Architect, Senior Technical Product Manager, Lead Engineer, Delivery Lead or senior technology consultant. More important than your exact title is your ability to combine technical breadth with delivery instinct.

# You will bring:
- Demonstrated experience leading or supporting complex technology delivery across multiple systems, stakeholders and delivery partners.
- The ability to move comfortably from business or service requirements into practical technical solutions, priorities and delivery tasks.
