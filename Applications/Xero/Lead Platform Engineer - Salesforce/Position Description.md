# Lead Platform Engineer - Salesforce

## Summary
Xero's Salesforce platform powers our go-to-market solutions and increasingly serves as the core infrastructure for our agents. You'll own the technical direction for how our platform is secured, released and operated, spending roughly half your time in hands-on engineering work: code, code reviews, design reviews, incident response and proofs of concept.

Your impact is measured in leverage. Today the platform runs 99.95% uptime, 100% path-to-production compliance, zero Sev 1 and Sev 2 incidents, and 150–200 deployments monthly. You'll maintain that bar whilst scaling significantly.

This role lets you Go Bold by challenging how the platform is built, Go Fast by removing friction to production, Go Further by building things that last, and Go Together by lifting capability across every pod that depends on you.

## The Team And How They Connect

The Platform team sits inside Customer Platforms, the engineering group behind Xero's go-to-market and customer experience technology. You'll work closely with engineering leaders across Security, Data, AI and with teams across Australia, New Zealand and offshore partners.


## The role will focus on

- Platform security posture. Access control, Connected Apps governance, vulnerability identification and remediation. You set standards and make secure the default.

- CI/CD and release engineering. Our deployment pipeline and release programmes. You champion automation and make shipping safe and fast for every engineer building on the platform.

- Platform observability and operational health. You grow observability, manage org limits, prevent customer-facing issues, lead incident response, and help shape how support shifts into our internal service model.

- The agent path-to-production. You'll build the route for agentic changes from idea to production safely: evaluation baselines, observability, an agent registry, and shared standards. This is a new area with no established playbook.

- Data governance and compliance. Partner with Data and Legal on GDPR deletion, retention, archival and data handling standards. Maintain and grow the D360 pipeline.

- Technical direction and enablement. Shape the platform's technical roadmap, partner with Product on technical investment ROI, contribute to Xero's engineering standards through RFCs and design docs, and identify capabilities your delivery teams need.

## Where And How You Can Work

Based in Melbourne; we encourage one day a week in the office to stay connected with your peers. The pods you support are distributed across Australia, New Zealand and offshore, so you're comfortable building trust and driving alignment asynchronously and in writing.

## The role and its impact

As a Platform Lead Engineer at Xero, you will partner with the Engineering Manager to set the technical vision for our Salesforce platform, then translate that vision into a clear, sequenced action plan. You'll own both the strategy and execution; setting direction in ambiguity and then delivering the plan to make it real. Your impact is measured less by features shipped and more by the leverage you create: how much safer, faster, and more reliable you make the teams that build on top of you.

You'll model the standard for engineering excellence across Xero, staying hands-on while building the technical capability and development practices of every pod that depends on the platform. This is a role where you'll Go Bold by challenging the status quo of our platform architecture, and Go Together by coaching and developing engineers across multiple disciplines.

## The Team And How They Connect

The Salesforce platform team provides the foundation that other engineering pods depend on to build and operate their solutions at scale. We work closely with product, security, data governance, and AI operations teams to ensure our platform is secure, performant, and ready for the next generation of capabilities.

## Here are some of the things we are looking for

- Technical leadership delivering and operating Salesforce at enterprise scale, with a platform engineering mindset
- Depth and breadth as a Salesforce developer, hands-on across code, reviews and production work
- Ownership of CI/CD pipelines and release engineering across major release cycles; GitHub, SFDX and test automation experience
- Proven track record owning security posture on platforms other teams depend on
- Experience leading incident response and turning post-incident reviews into lasting changes
- The ability to make code and design decisions across multiple teams, and to lead architecture discussions that reach a decision
- Strong written technical communication—RFCs, design docs and post-incident reviews to create alignment through persuasion, not authority
- Fluent use of AI tooling in your own engineering, evolving your practice as tools change
- Coaching ability with senior engineers; a force multiplier through code review standards and practices you set
- Partnership with Product—turning business goals into technical plans and pushing back on unclear scope


## The role will focus on

- Technical Vision: Working with the EM to refine the technical vision for the platform, identifying the concrete steps to achieving that vision, and breaking these down to a granular level, so that these are executable by engineers in Q4 and subsequent quarters.
- Platform security: Own the cross-cutting security posture across the Salesforce platform, including access control frameworks, Connected Apps governance, vulnerability identification and remediation, and security vendor and tooling decisions.
- CI/CD and release engineering: Own the deployment pipeline and release-engineering programme across our major release cycles each year. Champion automation, ensure pipelines are fit for purpose, and improve the developer experience for engineers shipping changes.
- Data governance and compliance: Own the platform side of data governance, including GDPR data deletion, retention and archival strategy, and the standards pods rely on for compliant data handling.
- Platform monitoring and limits: Maintain and grow observability across the platform, proactively manage org limits and platform-level production risk, and prevent customer-facing problems before they occur.
- D360 pipeline: Own the maintenance and growth of the D360 pipeline as it relates to Salesforce, and its integration with the rest of the platform.
- AI operations: Set direction for the platform's emerging AI operations agenda, including agent registry, observability, and evaluation baselines, and alignment of Agentforce agents to shared standards. Shape the approach where there is no established playbook, then build the plan to deliver it.
- Engineering standards and enablement: Contribute to Xero's engineering standards and frameworks, and work closely with engineering leadership to apply them across the platform and the pods that build on it. Identify capabilities that need to be grown and enable delivery teams to adopt them.
