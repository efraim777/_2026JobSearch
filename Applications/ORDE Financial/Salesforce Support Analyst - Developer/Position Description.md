## Who are we looking for?

At ORDE, we are looking for a Salesforce Support Analyst / Developer who has experience working in financial services industry to join our team. As the Salesforce Developer, you will play a key role in maintaining the Symphonix Origination and Loan Servicing packages on Salesforce platform and addressing ongoing business user issues. You will be part of an evolving team, working closely with 2 other team members of the Salesforce Platform Support Team and the Enterprise Tech Ops team. You will be also delivering quick wins as part of the Salesforce Platform Support Team’s responsibilities.

## Your key responsibilities will include:

- Provide Level 1 and Level 2 support for Symphonix (Q2) Origination and Loan Servicing users on Salesforce platform, that involves troubleshooting of user-reported problems and/or resolving configuration or data-related errors.
VMonitor and maintain platform health, ensuring system availability using Datadog.
- Manage user access and permissions, including role hierarchies, profiles, permission sets, licenses and other Salesforce UAM related aspects adhering to least privileges principles.
- Collaborate with Credit and Loan servicing business teams that use Symphonix packages, to understand issues, document requirements for fixes or enhancements, and escalate complex problems to development teams when necessary.
- Coordinating with project delivery teams for feature/bug handovers, debugging and knowledge transfers where needed.
- Configure and update Salesforce config such as Apex, Flows, Validation Rules, Page Layouts, LWC etc to address problems raised through support tickets.
- Configure and/or update Symphonix Loan servicing configuration issues that involves debugging and fixing payment clearing, interest posting, loan health etc.
- Configure and/or update Symphonix Origination Configuration issues that involves debugging and fixing issues during the application creation / assessment process.
- Create, maintain and use SOPs to support recurring support tickets like data management, UAM, accounting issues from Symphonix, user errors etc.
- Using JSM to manage support tickets and SOPs as knowledge.
- Collaborate with Salesforce developers, testers, and product owners to ensure smooth handover of fixes and enhancements from development to production.
